Roche Lab Variant Viewer

Under construction v1.0.5-alpha.1 · June 2026

I'm hoping to add certain features, improve speed and user experience, and remove bugs.

  • First look at a gene? Allow 1–5 minutes — the server collects variant data from ClinVar, GnomAD, and Ensembl, calculates molecular consequences, and builds the charts from scratch.
  • Seen it before? Much faster — variant and consequence data stays cached on the server after the first request.
  • Found a bug, or have an idea? Reach out at alexander_lehr@brown.edu.
